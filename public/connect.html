How to scan

  1. Open WhatsApp

    On the phone that will act as the Imisoro assistant line.

  2. Go to Linked Devices

    Tap the menu, then Settings, then Linked Devices. On iPhone it is under Settings directly.

  3. Tap Link a Device

    Point your camera at the QR code on this page.

  4. Wait for the green state

    Once connected, the assistant is live and ready to answer DMs.

Need help? The session is stored locally on this machine only. No one else sees your QR or your messages.
